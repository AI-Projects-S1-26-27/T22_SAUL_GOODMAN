How a trial works

  1. Two lawyers argue

    The Accuser says it happened. The Defender says it didn't. Each one shows evidence, like a photo, a text message or a witness.

  2. Unfair evidence gets thrown out

    A rumour, a fake photo, something taken illegally: the other lawyer can object. If the judge agrees, that evidence stops counting.

  3. The judge is a rulebook

    There's no AI on the bench. The judge follows fixed rules and weighs only accepted evidence. Same case, same choices, same verdict, every time.
